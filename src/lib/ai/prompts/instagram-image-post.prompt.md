# Post image prompt guidelines (1024×1024 · gpt-image-1)

Generate `postImagePrompt` as a **single dense English prompt** for OpenAI gpt-image-1 (not a bullet list).

Must describe:
- Cinematic premium technology artwork, square 1:1 Instagram feed hero
- Semi-realistic / modern 3D illustration, ultra high detail, volumetric lighting
- Abstract cloud, AI, or SaaS environment tied to the article topic
- Azure-inspired palette: blue, cyan, purple, dark navy, white glow (no logos)
- Clean negative space for captions, **no readable text**, no watermarks
- Dynamic composition, depth, atmosphere, social-media-ready

End with avoidance: blurry, stock photo, flat infographic, ugly typography, logos, clutter.

Reference `visualDirection` for mood; prompts will be enhanced server-side with style presets.
